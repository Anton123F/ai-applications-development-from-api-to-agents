# Style Guide Examples (Empathetic Mentor)

## Email Subject Lines

| Before (❌) | After (✅) | Rule Applied |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| URGENT: Action required on your account immediately | Let's get your account set up together ✨ | Remove fake urgency; replace with supportive partnership |
| Product Update v4.2 Release Notes | A few gentle updates to make your day easier | Focus on human benefit rather than technical versioning |
| Don't miss out on our webinar today | You're invited: Let's talk about finding balance | Warm, invitational tone instead of FOMO pressure |
| Error: Failed login attempt detected | Need a hand getting back in? Let's fix it | Empathy-first approach to troubleshooting |

## Email Body Rewrites

### Support Email
**Before (❌):**
> Dear Customer, your support ticket #4921 has been reviewed. The system error you encountered is due to invalid user parameters. Please rectify the input data in your settings panel and resubmit the form. Failure to do so will result in persistent task termination. Regards, Support Team.

**After (✅):**
> Hi there, I saw you ran into a bit of trouble with your settings earlier today—that's never fun! It looks like a couple of the details in your form just need a quick refresh. Whenever you have a moment, let's head over to your settings panel, update those fields, and try submitting again. If it still gives you any trouble, just reply right here and I'll take a closer look for you! Rooting for you, Support Team.

**Changes:**
- Removed cold, robotic corporate phrasing and technical jargon.
- Added an empathetic opening acknowledging the user's frustration.
- Used contractions (*that's*, *I'll*) and collaborative partnering language (*let's head over*, *I'm here*).
- Concluded with warm, reassuring support and an open door.

## Social Media Rewrites

### LinkedIn Post
**Before (❌):**
> We are thrilled to announce the release of our Q3 feature update. Users can now optimize their workflow efficiency by 40% utilizing our new automation protocols. Click the link below to deploy this update. #SaaS #Productivity #BusinessGrowth

**After (✅):**
> Ever feel like there just aren't enough hours in the day? 💛 We've been listening to your feedback, and we're so excited to share a few gentle updates designed to give you some breathing room. By automating the repetitive little tasks, you can save up to a few hours each week for the work you actually love. Take a peek at how it works when you're ready. 🌱 #Productivity #WorkSmarter #MindfulWork

**Changes:**
- Replaced corporate self-praise (*"We are thrilled"*) with a relatable, empathetic hook question.
- Swapped clinical metrics (*"optimize workflow efficiency by 40%"*) with human-centric benefits (*"give you some breathing room"*).
- Added warm, subtle emojis and community-focused hashtags.

### Twitter/X Post
**Before (❌):**
> System maintenance scheduled for 02:00 UTC. Expect service disruption. Apologies for any inconvenience caused. 

**After (✅):**
> Quick heads-up: We're doing a bit of housekeeping tonight at 2:00 UTC to keep things running smoothly. Things might be quiet for a brief moment, but we'll be right back before you know it! Thanks for bearing with us. ✨

**Changes:**
- Softened formal system downtime language into a friendly, casual heads-up (*"housekeeping tonight"*).
- Replaced cold corporate apologies with warm appreciation (*"Thanks for bearing with us"*).

## Support Reply Rewrites

**Before (❌):**
> Your request cannot be processed because the file format is incorrect. Accepted formats are .pdf and .docx only. Please re-upload a compliant file.

**After (✅):**
> Oh no, it looks like that file format didn't quite want to cooperate! No worries at all—our system is currently set up to accept `.pdf` and `.docx` files. Whenever you're ready, feel free to drop one of those over, and let's get your document processed together! Let me know if you need any help converting it.
